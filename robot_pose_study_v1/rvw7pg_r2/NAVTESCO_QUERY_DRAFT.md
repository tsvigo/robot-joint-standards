**Subject: Technical feasibility query — 4× RVW-7PG / RVW-7PA with radial axes
on a Ø620 mm circular platform (medical logistics AGV), Rev.005.1 data**

Dear Nabtesco Precision Europe team,

we are evaluating four RVW units for a circular indoor AGV and would appreciate
your technical guidance on the following points.

**A. Current documentation (Rev.005.1)**
1. Our available documents disagree on the RVW-7PG allowable
   acceleration/deceleration torque: **16 N·m** (Product Guide, PDF 10/2026,
   spec table for 7PG) vs **27 N·m** (RVW Series catalogue Rev.004.1, 2020/2023,
   rating table; also your product web page). Please confirm the **values valid
   in Rev.005.1** for RVW-7PG (rated / allowable acc.-dec. / momentary max.),
   and the applicable duty/time limits for each value.
2. Please also confirm the current total length C and mass of the RVW-7PG main
   unit (Rev.004.1: C = 124 mm; Product Guide: C = 118 mm) and whether
   backlash/lost motion is 10 or 12 arc.min. in Rev.005.1.
3. Is the full Rev.005.1 catalogue (including actuator types) available for
   download, or may we receive it by e-mail?

**B. RVW-7PA actuator (BLV Series R Type / Oriental Motor)**
4. Specifications of RVW-7PALA-30-0GO1 / -0GO2: overall dimensions with motor,
   mass of the complete motorised assembly, rated/peak torque, motor rated
   speed, and the availability of a **fail-safe (spring-applied) parking brake**
   (brake holding torque and voltage), encoder type and resolution.
For reference, our calculation of the required parking-hold torque at the
   wheel output (ideal model, 450 kg, 8 % slope, worst roller direction) is
   approx. **11.1 N·m per wheel** — which brake holding torque do you recommend?
5. Cooling and mounting requirements: is forced cooling needed for continuous
   duty at wheel torque ≈5–10 N·m? Which mounting orientations are allowed
   (radial axes pointing to the vehicle centre)? Are there mounting tolerance
   recommendations for the module interface?
6. Is an RVW-7PA configuration with **torsion code R** (right barrel) available,
   so that four identical units (RRRR or LLLL) can be ordered?
7. May we receive the STEP data for RVW-7PALA-30-0GO1 and the Rev.005.1
   catalogue (download portal currently requires member login)?

**C. Radial mounting with four identical wheels**
8. Is a configuration with four **radial wheel axes** (pointing to the platform
   centre, 4-fold symmetric) supported by RVW-7PG/7PA? Any documented
   restrictions compared with the standard rectangular layouts in the catalogue?
9. For LLLL/RRRR torsion combinations: any application restrictions? Do they
   require specific drive-control kinematics compared with a rectangular X
   layout?
10. Axial loads: our model shows axial force components in the wheel/roller
    bearings during sideways and diagonal motions. What axial load limits apply
    to RVW-7PG, and how do they affect rated life?
11. Medical application: per your catalogue notice, products used in medical
    equipment require individual examination — which checks/documentation are
    required for a patient-adjacent hospital environment?

Environment: ambient −10…+40 °C; indoor linoleum; total mass scenarios
300/400/450 kg (below your 3× rule for four wheels of 200 kgf each).

Kind regards,
[customer]
